# XENONRP ROLEPLAY RULEBOOK

This rulebook applies to XenonRP players across in-game roleplay and community spaces. XenonRP’s current activity, faction, and platform policies govern any details they publish separately. If a scene is unclear, keep it fair, avoid escalation, and ask staff through the official support channel.

## GENERAL RULES

- Use only music, video, images, and other media that you have permission to use when playing, streaming, or sharing XenonRP content. Follow the rules of the platform you publish on.
- Use a working microphone and keep voice communication understandable. Do not use background audio, soundboards, or voice effects to disrupt scenes or drown out other players.
- Stay in an active roleplay scene until it reaches a reasonable conclusion. Do not disconnect or switch out of character to avoid its consequences. If a technical problem or urgent safety issue interrupts play, explain it through the official support process and return to the scene when directed.
- Treat players and staff respectfully. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, slurs, threats, and targeted conduct are prohibited both in and out of character. Roleplay is not permission to direct real-world abuse at another person.
- Do not use voice, messages, emotes, or public posts to humiliate, harass, or provoke another player. Keep competitive taunts and personal disputes out of scenes.
- Do not advertise or recruit for other game servers or communities in XenonRP spaces without staff approval.
- Players are responsible for reading the current XenonRP rules and applicable activity or faction policies. A rule change takes effect when published; not knowing a rule does not excuse a violation.

## CHARACTER RULES

- Use a believable character identity that complies with XenonRP’s character and whitelist requirements. Do not use impersonating, deliberately disruptive, or otherwise disallowed names.
- Keep roleplay in character. Do not introduce out-of-character chat, modern player slang, or information your character could not know into an active scene. Use OOC channels only where the server permits them.
- If a disagreement arises during a scene, continue safely where possible and report it afterward through the official support channel. Do not use OOC arguments to force the scene to stop or decide its outcome.
- Do not deliberately bait, troll, grief, or manipulate another player into breaking roleplay or receiving a punishment.
- Value your character’s life. Respond to credible threats and serious danger in a believable way; do not act invulnerable or ignore a clear disadvantage just to win a confrontation.

## CHEATING & BUG ABUSE

### Cheats, modifications, and automation

- Cheats, mod menus, unauthorized scripts, macros, input automation, and third-party tools that alter gameplay or provide an unfair advantage are prohibited. Do not use automation to bypass inactivity systems or perform repeated in-game actions.
- Visual or accessibility changes are allowed only when they comply with XenonRP’s current client and platform requirements. Do not remove, hide, or alter objects, effects, or other information to gain an advantage; do not manipulate resolution, aspect ratio, or display settings to see through cover or improve combat unfairly.
- Do not evade a restriction or ban by changing accounts, clients, or tools.

### Bugs and exploits

- Do not knowingly exploit a client, server, map, or gameplay bug for advantage. Stop using the affected feature and report the issue privately through XenonRP’s official support channel.
- Do not share exploit instructions, help others reproduce an exploit for gain, or keep assets or progress obtained through a bug. Follow staff instructions about evidence and remediation.
- Staff may request relevant recordings or other evidence when reviewing a report. Players should preserve and provide evidence they have; staff POV or recordings are not guaranteed to be available.
- Sanctions depend on the evidence and severity under XenonRP’s enforcement process. Do not assume that a specific punishment or appeal outcome is automatic.

## ROLEPLAY RULES

### Metagaming

Metagaming is using information your character did not learn in the roleplay world to influence in-character decisions. This includes information from Discord, streams, social media, OOC conversations, or another character or account. Keep that information out of your character’s choices, and do not pass scene details through external channels to affect active play.

### Powergaming

Powergaming is forcing an outcome, taking an impossible action, or denying another player a fair chance to respond. Keep actions plausible for the character and situation. Describe actions and injuries fairly, respect reasonable responses, and do not exploit game mechanics to achieve something that could not happen in the scene.

### Combat logging and combat storing

- Do not leave the server, switch characters, or otherwise disconnect to evade an active scene or its consequences.
- If a crash or connection failure prevents you from staying, reconnect as soon as practical and report the interruption through the official support channel. Do not use a claimed crash as cover for a deliberate exit.
- During an active conflict, do not hide, transfer, or move valuables into inaccessible inventories or storage to avoid a roleplay consequence.

### RDM and VDM

- Random Deathmatch (RDM) is attacking or killing without a valid roleplay reason. Establish a meaningful in-character context before using violence and give others a fair opportunity to understand and respond, except where a published XenonRP faction procedure governs the encounter.
- Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM) is deliberately using a vehicle to injure or kill without a valid roleplay basis. Do not use vehicles as weapons or exploit vehicle mechanics to force an unfair result.

### Combat initiation and third-party involvement

Combat must follow from a believable roleplay situation, not an out-of-character grudge or a desire to farm fights. Where the scene allows, communicate a clear, audible in-character cue and give the other party a reasonable chance to react. Do not rely on a fixed phrase or use a warning to excuse an otherwise invalid attack. Law-enforcement use-of-force and other faction-specific situations follow XenonRP’s published procedures. Do not bring uninvolved players or groups into a conflict without a valid in-character reason and permission under the applicable activity rules.

## NEW LIFE RULE

- Follow the current XenonRP death, incapacitation, and respawn rules. When those rules require your character to forget a scene, do not return to it, seek revenge, or act on information your character no longer remembers.
- Do not pressure or coerce another player into choosing a particular respawn, memory, or roleplay outcome. Apply only the requirements stated in XenonRP’s current rules and game mechanics.
- Do not use Discord, streams, or other out-of-character communication to summon friends, arrange a rescue, or pass information about an active scene.
- Death or incapacitation does not make a player an unrestricted target. Respect the active scene and the current rules for medical care, looting, and respawn.

## ROBBING & TORTURE

### Robberies and hostages

- A robbery must have a reasonable in-character basis and play out as a scene; do not rob players at random or use a robbery solely to provoke a fight.
- Do not use AFK, incapacitated, or otherwise unavailable players as convenient hostages. Do not stage fake hostages or use an agreed accomplice to manufacture leverage.
- Do not target protected service roles, spawn points, or designated safe areas as hostage locations unless a XenonRP rule expressly permits it. Follow any activity-specific hostage and participant limits published by XenonRP.
- A hostage scene must leave the people involved a reasonable opportunity to participate. Do not use OOC pressure to dictate another player’s response.

### Torture and sensitive roleplay

- Torture or similarly intense roleplay requires a valid scene and the other participant’s explicit out-of-character consent before it begins. Consent may be withdrawn at any time; stop or redirect the scene immediately when asked.
- Keep sensitive content within XenonRP and platform rules. Sexual violence, real-world threats, and prohibited or non-consensual content are not allowed.
- Preserve and provide relevant evidence if staff request it through the official review process. Do not fabricate or alter evidence.

## COMMUNICATION

- Use in-game OOC chat only for a necessary technical issue or where XenonRP staff explicitly permit it. Do not use it for arguments, competitive remarks, reports, or sharing information your character should not know.
- Submit player reports and staff concerns through XenonRP’s listed support or ticket channels. Include the approximate time, people involved, a clear account of what happened, and any relevant clips or screenshots you have.
- Report incidents as soon as reasonably possible while evidence is available. Any specific reporting deadline published by XenonRP for a particular process takes precedence; this rulebook does not create a separate deadline.
- Do not pressure staff through personal direct messages or public callouts. Keep reports confidential while they are reviewed and respond to reasonable requests for information.
- Do not make knowingly false, misleading, or retaliatory reports. Report what you observed and distinguish it from what you infer.

## ROLEPLAY QUALITY

### Respectful, scene-focused play

- Do not bait, stalk, or deliberately distract law enforcement or other public-service roleplayers. Use calls and service tools for a genuine in-character purpose, not to disrupt an active scene.
- Cooperate with medical roleplay when it applies. Roleplay injuries consistently, allow emergency responders to do their work, and do not force instant treatment or use medical mechanics to evade a scene.
- Do not repeatedly shoot downed players, spam microphones or chat, use disrespectful emotes to provoke them, interrupt scenes for attention, or turn ordinary interactions into public drama.
- Keep disputes and criticism factual and use the official support process. Harassment or targeted abuse of the server, staff, or other players is not an acceptable substitute for a report.

### Fair and believable roleplay

Low-quality roleplay includes ignoring reasonable actions communicated through the server’s roleplay tools, acting unaffected by serious injuries, communicating normally while incapacitated where the rules prohibit it, spectating or joining an active fight without a roleplay reason, or using an item or mechanic in a way that defeats its intended roleplay purpose. It also includes repeatedly forcing the same combat scene, using vehicles or map features in implausible ways, or interfering with another player’s property without a scene basis. Give others room to participate and react.

- Do not treat XenonRP as an arena or pursue fights solely for kills, clips, or a win-at-all-costs result. Combat must serve the character and the scene.
- Do not use stretched or distorted display settings, unauthorized crosshairs, or other external aids to gain a combat advantage. Follow XenonRP’s current client requirements.
- Recordings and montages must comply with privacy, platform, and community rules. Do not edit or publish content to harass, threaten, or target another player.
- Sensitive or sexual roleplay must remain consensual, private, and within XenonRP and platform requirements. Stop if a participant withdraws consent.

## COMBAT

- Start combat only when it follows from a credible roleplay situation. Do not manufacture shootouts, repeatedly provoke law enforcement, or use violence as a shortcut to avoid ordinary roleplay consequences.
- Law enforcement activity follows XenonRP’s current police and faction procedures. Apply any participant and engagement limits published for the specific activity.
- Use force proportionately to the scene. Do not shoot solely to loot, punish, or provoke another player. Follow any activity-specific escalation and disengagement rules XenonRP publishes.
- Do not move, pose, or otherwise exploit injured or dead players to taunt them or create disrespectful content. Finish only the roleplay actions the scene permits and leave the area when appropriate.
- Third parties may join only when they have a valid in-character reason and the applicable XenonRP activity rules allow it.
- Do not use vehicle trunks, collision quirks, repeated ramming, or other vehicle mechanics as unfair combat cover or an exploit.
- Do not take phones or other protected items where XenonRP’s current mechanics or rules prohibit it. Looting and property transfer must remain within the scene and applicable rules; do not use them to evade a death, respawn, or investigation consequence.

## CRIMINAL ACTIVITIES

- Criminal activity must be driven by roleplay and comply with XenonRP’s current laws, faction policies, event rules, and published participant limits. Follow the limits for the specific activity; do not assume a limit that has not been published.
- Plan crimes and conflicts without metagaming, exploit use, or out-of-character coordination. Do not bring extra participants or switch characters to bypass an activity limit.
- Hostages must be genuine participants in a valid scene. Do not use friends, AFK players, incapacitated people, or service personnel as props; follow XenonRP’s current hostage restrictions and leave room for a fair response.
- Do not start or continue a conflict in a location XenonRP designates as protected or safe. Do not drag an active hostile scene into a medical or law-enforcement facility to block service or force an advantage.
- Do not claim or camp a shared activity, processing, or crafting location as permanently controlled. Use location-specific rules where they exist; otherwise, interact fairly and do not start violence without a roleplay basis.
- Organized fights and conflicts are allowed only where the current XenonRP rules expressly permit them and within the limits those rules set.

## WARNING SYSTEM

- XenonRP staff assess violations using the available evidence, severity, context, prior history, and impact on other players. Outcomes may range from guidance or a warning to removal, suspension, or a permanent ban; serious misconduct may warrant immediate action.
- The number of warnings, if any, depends on the incident and staff review. Serious misconduct may lead to immediate action under XenonRP’s enforcement process.
- Players are expected to know the current rules. Being unfamiliar with a rule does not prevent staff from taking action.
- Be respectful and truthful during a review. Intimidating staff, interfering with an investigation, or submitting fabricated evidence may lead to further action.
- Use XenonRP’s official appeal or support process if you believe an enforcement decision was made in error. Do not attempt to relitigate a case through public arguments or personal messages to staff.
- XenonRP may update this rulebook and publish separate activity or faction rules. The published version and official notices determine which rules apply.
